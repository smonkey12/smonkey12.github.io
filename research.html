Research & Publications

My work combines statistical and optimization theory with algorithm development and empirical study to understand modern AI systems.

Foundations of Large Language Models

I study how optimization and training shape transformer behavior, with particular interests in implicit bias, in-context learning, attention mechanisms, and generalization.

Interpretability of AI

I develop methods for understanding learned representations and model decisions, including feature recovery with sparse autoencoders and mechanistic analysis of transformer reasoning.

Synthetic Data and Post-Training

I study when synthetic data improve pretrained models and how verification, generation noise, selection, and feedback affect subsequent learning. I am also interested in adaptive data generation and agentic post-training.

I am interested in connecting these theoretical questions with efficient and reliable AI methods for scientific and societal applications.
